Class 1 English
Lesson: The Short A Sound

1. Learning the Sound

Today we will learn the short a sound. The short a sound is heard in words
like cat, mat, hat, bag, fan, and apple. When we say cat, the middle sound is
/a/. Students should listen carefully and repeat the sound after the teacher.

3. Simple Sentences

A cat is on the mat.
The hat is red.
I have a bag.
The fan is on.
I can see an apple.

4. Reading Practice

Read each word slowly. First say the sounds, then say the whole word.

c - a - t, cat
m - a - t, mat
h - a - t, hat
b - a - g, bag
f - a - n, fan

5. Classroom Activity

The teacher shows picture cards or draws simple pictures on the board: a cat,
a mat, a hat, a bag, and a fan. Students say the word aloud and clap when they
hear the short a sound.

Then students work in pairs. One student points to a word. The other student
reads the word and uses it in a simple sentence.

6. Blackboard Work

Short a sound: /a/
Words: cat, mat, hat, bag, fan
Sentence: A cat is on the mat.

7. Quick Questions

1. Which sound do you hear in cat?
2. Which word rhymes with cat: mat or dog?
3. Read this word: bag.
4. Make a sentence with hat.
5. Circle the short a word: sun, map, pen.

8. Homework

Write five short a words.
Draw one picture for a short a word.
Read this sentence to someone at home: A cat is on the mat.
