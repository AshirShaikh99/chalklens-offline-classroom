# Demo and Video Storyboard

## Video Goal

The video is the most important submission asset.

It must show:

- A real problem.
- A specific user.
- A working app.
- Gemma 4 doing something meaningful.
- A clear positive impact.

Maximum length: 3 minutes.

## Core Story

A teacher in a low-resource classroom has one phone, one textbook, weak internet, and students who need clearer explanations.

She scans a page. ChalkLens turns it into a simple classroom lesson, blackboard notes, quiz, and activity offline.

## Story Arc

### 0:00-0:20 - Problem

Visual:

- Teacher looking at textbook.
- Students in class.
- Phone shows weak/no internet.

Narration:

> In many classrooms, the problem is not that children are unwilling to learn. The problem is that the lesson is not reaching them.

### 0:20-0:40 - User Pain

Show:

- Formal textbook paragraph.
- Teacher has limited prep time.
- Students confused by difficult vocabulary.

Narration:

> A teacher may have 50 students, one textbook, weak internet, and very little time to turn dense textbook text into teachable material.

### 0:40-1:20 - App Demo

Show:

1. Open ChalkLens.
2. Open New Lesson.
3. Import the sample fruits PDF.
4. Review the extracted source text.
5. Generate the lesson kit.

Narration:

> ChalkLens uses Gemma 4 locally to read the uploaded lesson page, infer the class and subject, and create a classroom-ready teaching kit.

### 1:20-1:55 - Output

Show:

- Simple explanation.
- Blackboard notes.
- Quiz.
- Local example.
- Easy version.
- Student help question.

Narration:

> The teacher gets the explanation, blackboard notes, oral questions, homework, and a simpler version for students who need more help.

### 1:55-2:20 - Technical Proof

Show:

- Offline mode.
- Gemma 4 local backend.
- Structured JSON.
- Local curriculum pack.
- Fine-tuning/benchmark comparison if ready.

Narration:

> It is not a cloud chatbot. It uses Gemma 4's multimodal understanding, structured generation, and local inference.

### 2:20-2:45 - Impact

Show:

- Teacher uses blackboard notes.
- Student answers a quiz question.
- App saved offline lesson.

Narration:

> One device helps the whole classroom. The teacher stays in control. Students get explanations they can actually follow.

### 2:45-3:00 - Closing

Narration:

> ChalkLens brings AI to the classroom that needs it most: offline, local, and built for teachers.

## Must-Have Shots

- No internet/offline indicator.
- Textbook page scan.
- Generated lesson kit.
- Plain-English explanation.
- Teacher using output.
- Student help mode.
- Technical architecture glimpse.

## Avoid

- Fake classroom disaster.
- Too much talking head.
- Long architecture explanation.
- Generic AI buzzwords.
- Overpromising replacement of teachers.

## Tagline Options

- One phone. One teacher. A whole classroom helped.
- AI lessons teachers can use immediately.
- Offline classroom intelligence for every teacher.
- From textbook page to classroom lesson in minutes.
